## Capability & Control

- **Capability outrunning control** — recursive acceleration meets inadequate oversight; a natural extension of the recursive self-improvement theme. Status: ready-to-write. See `ft-topics.md` for evidence log.

## Economic Agency & Market Access

- **AI agents as market gatekeepers: small business access displacement** (`ft-1vl`) — Cloudflare CEO (Jun 2026): AI could "destroy small businesses" by making it harder to persuade agents that intermediate purchasing. Market access displacement as a distinct form of power asymmetry — beyond labour displacement. Who controls agent preferences? Community economic agency in an agent-mediated market. The 1891 parallel: new intermediaries reshaping market participation before frameworks to respond have solidified.

## AI Access & Geopolitics

- **AI access as geopolitical resource: differential frontier access by political alignment** — Both OpenAI (GPT-5.6 Sol, ~20 vetted partners) and Anthropic (Mythos 5, Fable 5, ~100 vetted U.S. orgs) now gate frontier models at U.S. government request. Austria's proposal to host Anthropic in the EU is the first government-level pushback. Core FT argument: frontier AI access is being structured with political access controls before communities understand what's being decided. The 1891 parallel applies. Abstract: `ft-abstracts/ai-access-geopolitical-resource.md`.

## Labour Displacement & Timeline

- **"We Must Act Now" — Stanford statement, 200+ economists/16 Nobel laureates on AI job-displacement timeline compression** — Signatories include lab insiders (Jeff Dean, Jack Clark, Noam Brown), not just outside critics. Core claim: prior transitions (steam, electricity, computers) gave decades to adapt; this one may give only a few years (Korinek, UVA). Strong fit for the 1891/window argument — but with the window itself explicitly compressed vs precedent. Abstract: `ft-abstracts/ai-jobs-timeline-we-must-act-now.md`.

## Epistemic Infrastructure

- **Accidental anonymity** — AI-polished professional output (resumes, portfolios, commit messages) erases the person behind it at scale. Not deception — optimisation. The competitive paradox: universal AI polish makes everyone indistinguishable. FT angle: communities need new signals for genuine human contribution. Abstract: `ft-abstracts/accidental-anonymity.md`.
