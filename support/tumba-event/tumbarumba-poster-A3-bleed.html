Is Tumbarumba Ready?

A free community talk on AI, work, and what’s coming next for the Tumbarumba region.

Whether you love technology or distrust it, the changes reshaping work, business and everyday life don’t wait for anyone’s opinion. The gap between what’s actually happening and what most people understand is enormous — and it’s growing. This is an honest community conversation — no jargon, no doom — with time for your questions.

What we’ll cover
⚡
The Timeline Has Collapsed
What we thought was years away is arriving now — and why the pace matters.
💼
Work & Local Business
Which roles are changing, what it means for regional economies, and what skills will matter.
🏡
Our Community’s Strengths
Why rural communities have real advantages in navigating this — if we move together.
🌅
What a Good Future Looks Like
Honest hope — the outcomes that are genuinely possible if we act.
✅
Six Things You Can Do
Practical actions, starting now, that make a real difference to you, your family, and your community.
