Free community workshop · Tumbarumba

Working together to prepare for emergencies

An EmergencyRedi preparedness workshop exploring practical, all-hazards preparation for major disruptions—from heatwaves and supply-chain shocks to rapid technological change and AI risks.

Hosted by Australian Red Cross · Supported locally by Future Together

WhenTuesday 15 September · 5–7 pm
WhereTumbarumba Bowling Club · Tumbarumba
EntryFree · All welcome
What you’ll take away
1

Start your Emergency RediPlan

Simple steps to prepare yourself, your household and the people you care about.

2

Prepare for changing conditions

An all-hazards approach for heat, supply issues and changing services.

3

Make sense of AI risks—together

Practical ways to recognise trusted information and strengthen local connections.

No jargon and no panic—just practical preparation, useful conversations and local connection.
