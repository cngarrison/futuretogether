📐 Folding instructions (triangle tent)

  1. Print on A4 paper — portrait, actual size (do not scale to fit).
  2. Fold inward along the top tuck line.
  3. Fold in half along the centre fold line, printed side out.
  4. Fold inward along the bottom tuck line.
  5. Tuck the two strips together inside to lock the triangle.

QR code links to futuretogether.community. Generated offline — no internet required to print.
