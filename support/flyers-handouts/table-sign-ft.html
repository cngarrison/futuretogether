📐 Folding instructions (triangle tent)

  1. Print on A4 paper — portrait, actual size (do not scale to fit).
  2. Score and fold inward along the top tuck line.
  3. Score and fold in half along the centre fold line, printed side out.
  4. Score and fold inward along the bottom tuck line.
  5. Tuck the two folded strips together inside to lock the triangular shape.
  6. Stand upright — each face points a different direction.

Guide lines print as faint hairlines and will be hidden inside the folds. Adjust --tuck: 24mm in the CSS to match your preferred fold depth.
