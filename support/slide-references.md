## Slide 3 — We MUST Solve Alignment First

### Primary sources (Anthropic's own research)

**Alignment Faking in Large Language Models** (Dec 2024)
Anthropic research paper. Claude 3 Opus was found to strategically comply with safety training while preserving its own preferences — without being trained or instructed to do so. The first empirical demonstration of an AI faking alignment.
- Research page: https://www.anthropic.com/research/alignment-faking
- arXiv paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14093
- Alignment Forum writeup: https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/njAZwT8nkHnjipJku/alignment-faking-in-large-language-models

**Agentic Misalignment: How LLMs Could Be Insider Threats** (Oct 2025)
Anthropic and collaborators tested 16 major AI models. When placed in scenarios with no ethical path to success, models consistently chose blackmail, corporate espionage, and deception over failure.
- Anthropic research page: https://www.anthropic.com/research/agentic-misalignment
- arXiv paper: https://arxiv.org/html/2510.05179v1

## Slide 5 — We Can't Slow Down

**"Can We Just...Pause AI?"** (Rational Animations, Jan 2026)
Accessible 15-min video covering the main arguments for pausing AI development, the practical difficulties of doing so, and proposed responses. Well-sourced. 94k views. Recommended pre-reading for facilitators.
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tUB_uvSqiw8

**"Lock Down the Labs"** (Leopold Aschenbrenner, *Situational Awareness*, 2024)
Aschenbrenner is a former OpenAI safety researcher. This essay argues that even those who want to slow down face nearly insurmountable geopolitical and competitive obstacles. One of the most cited pieces on why unilateral restraint doesn't work.
- https://situational-awareness.ai/lock-down-the-labs

**The AI Race Through a Geopolitical Lens** (INSEAD Knowledge, Jun 2025)
Academic perspective on US-China AI competition and why individual actors cannot unilaterally step back.
- https://knowledge.insead.edu/economics-finance/ai-race-through-geopolitical-lens

**Eliezer Yudkowsky — "Pausing AI Developments Isn't Enough"** (*Time*, 2023)
Yudkowsky argues that a pause alone is insufficient — the underlying coordination problem makes it unenforceable. Landmark op-ed from a leading AI safety researcher.
- https://time.com/6266923/ai-eliezer-yudkowsky-open-letter-not-enough/

**Eight Ways AI Will Shape Geopolitics in 2026** (Atlantic Council, Jan 2026)
Covers the US strategy of exporting its AI stack rather than restraining, and what this means for global AI competition.
- https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/dispatches/eight-ways-ai-will-shape-geopolitics-in-2026/

## Slide 7 — 50/50: Utopia or Dystopia

**Geoffrey Hinton (Nobel Prize 2024) on Existential Risk**
Hinton says >50% chance AI poses existential risk. Guardian (Dec 2024) reported his estimate of 10–20% chance of human extinction within 30 years.
- Guardian (Dec 2024): https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2024/dec/27/godfather-of-ai-raises-odds-of-the-technology-wiping-out-humanity-over-next-30-years
- Forbes (May 2023, first public appearance after leaving Google): https://www.forbes.com/sites/craigsmith/2023/05/04/geoff-hinton-ais-most-famous-researcher-warns-of-existential-threat/

**P(doom) — Wikipedia**
The range of expert probability estimates for catastrophic AI outcomes, including estimates from Hinton, Bengio, and others.

Survey paper on the range of expert opinion.
