## Option B - Longer, warmer community version

Feeling a bit unsure about phones, tablets or computers? You're not alone - and you're welcome to come along and learn together.

Future Together is hosting **Get Online Together**, a free, friendly session as part of national Get Online Week 2026. We'll be at Tumbarumba Public Library on **Friday 23 October, 10:30am-12pm**, with morning tea, a few prizes, and a relaxed, judgement-free atmosphere.

There's no experience needed - whether you want help with text messages, video calls to family, online banking safety, or just getting more comfortable with your device, our volunteers will be there to help at your own pace. Just bring along your phone, tablet or laptop (or come and watch, that's fine too!).

This event is part of the national Get Online Week, supported by Good Things Australia, and run locally by Future Together - because getting online is easier, and friendlier, when we do it together.

**Spaces are limited, so please register:**
- Online: futuretogether.community/meetups
- Phone: 02 6948 3707
- Email: info@futuretogether.community
