Participant information

Get Online Together: Digital Skills for Tumbarumba · Fri 23 Oct 2026, 10:30am–12pm AEDT

Our approach

This free, friendly session is for local people aged 50+ to explore Be Connected learning, online safety and emergency information. You choose what to try. Please ask for help at any time; you do not have to share private information to take part.

Photos: two separate choices

Public photos — opt in only.
We may invite you to opt in to photos that could be used publicly (for example, on Future Together channels or event promotion). You can say no or change your mind. Your choice will not affect your participation.
Respectful delivery evidence / acquittal images.
We may take respectful images showing the event being delivered — such as the room, materials, activities and attendee numbers — for reporting and grant acquittal. We will minimise identifiable faces where possible and will not photograph personal screens, surveys, prize slips or sign-in sheets. These images are not described as anonymous.
Before a photo is taken, ask what it is for. Tell a facilitator if you do not wish to be photographed or if you need your choice clarified.

Be Connected signup and device help

A Be Connected account may be offered as an option; it is not required to attend or receive help. If you choose to sign up, review the information requested and enter your own details. Helpers can talk you through steps but should not ask for, record or keep your password, multi-factor code, bank details, identity documents or recovery codes. You can pause or ask for a different way to learn.

Online safety and support

If you receive a suspicious message, pause before clicking, replying or paying. Contact the organisation using a phone number or website you already know. For a scam, consider Scamwatch; if identity information may have been exposed, IDCARE may help. For immediate danger, call 000.

Prize draw terms

No purchase necessary. Entry is voluntary and open to attendees aged 18+ who submit a first name/contact method on a prize slip by 11:55am on 23 October 2026. One entry per person. Prizes are not transferable or redeemable for cash. The draw will occur at the event at about 11:55am. A winner not present may be contacted using the supplied details; if there is no response within 14 days, a redraw may occur. Contact details are used only to administer the draw and then securely deleted. Staff and immediate family may not enter.

Questions or concerns

Speak with a facilitator during the session, or contact Future Together: 02 6948 3707 · info@futuretogether.community. Event information: futuretogether.community/meetups.
