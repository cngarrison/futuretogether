Get Online
Together

Digital skills for everyday confidence

A friendly, hands-on session for local people aged 50+

Get more out of the technology you already have. Join a welcoming, hands-on session for local people aged 50+—with practical guidance, time to try things yourself, and friendly support when you need it. Bring your phone, tablet or laptop; no experience is needed.

What we’ll cover
🧭
Be Connected
Find free, trusted learning resources and practical support that fit your interests and goals.
🔒
Stay safe online
Learn simple ways to spot scams, create stronger passwords and add an extra layer of protection to important accounts.
📣
Emergency alerts
Know where to find reliable local warnings, updates and emergency information when it matters.
💬
Friendly help
Ask the questions that matter to you and practise at your own pace, with patient one-to-one support.
🚀
Put your skills into action
Leave with six practical things you can do straight away—from connecting safely and finding information to helping yourself, family and community stay in touch.
