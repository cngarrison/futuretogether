The Future Is Arriving. Is Tumbarumba Ready?
A Future Together public talk · Café Nest Cinema, Tumbarumba

What we covered tonight

  • AI capabilities are advancing far faster than most people realise — what seemed 10 years away is arriving in 1–3 years
  • The people building AI admit they haven't solved how to make it reliably safe
  • White-collar and creative jobs are already being displaced — not just in cities
  • Rural communities have fewer fallback options when industries shift
  • Experts put the risk of a very bad outcome at somewhere between 10–50%. Nobody credible says zero.
  • There's an equally real possibility of an extraordinary positive future — cancer solved, expertise available everywhere
  • The path we get depends on what we do now

Questions to start a conversation

Partners & friends
  • Did you know AI is already doing high-level legal and medical work? How do you think that affects us?
  • What would you do if your job looked completely different in three years?
  • If there's even a 20% chance of major disruption — what would we do differently?
Kids & teenagers
  • What do you think jobs will look like when you grow up?
  • Have you used AI for anything lately? What did you think?
  • If a computer could do most jobs, what would people do all day?
Local businesses & colleagues
  • Which parts of what we do could AI handle in the next few years?
  • What's one thing about our work that AI couldn't replace?
  • Are we preparing for this — or waiting to react?

Not panic. Not paralysis. Action.
